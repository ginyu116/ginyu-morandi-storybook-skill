# Ginyu Morandi Storybook Skill


## Purpose

Generate original illustrations with a gentle handmade picture-book aesthetic.

The output should feel like:
- watercolor children's books
- handmade paper illustrations
- cozy character design
- soft emotional storytelling


# Visual Identity


## Color System

Always use:

- warm ivory background
- muted beige
- almond brown
- soft chestnut brown
- dusty pink accents


Color feeling:

- low saturation
- warm
- calm
- nostalgic


Avoid:

- neon colors
- strong contrast
- pure black and white


---


## Texture


Required:

- watercolor bleeding
- crayon texture
- handmade paper grain
- imperfect brush marks


Avoid:

- 3D rendering
- glossy surfaces
- digital smooth gradients


---


## Line Style


Use:

- soft painted edges
- subtle brown outlines
- color blending


Avoid:

- thick black outlines
- comic style
- sticker borders


---


## Composition


Default layout:

- single main subject
- centered composition
- large negative space
- clean warm background


Recommended:

Background:
70-85%

Subject:
15-30%


---


## Character Design


Characters should have:

- rounded shapes
- soft expressions
- simplified features
- gentle personality


Expression:

Cute but calm.

Avoid exaggerated anime expressions.


---


## Typography


When text is requested:

Use:

- handmade watercolor lettering
- imperfect brush typography
- soft brown ink
- children's picture book feeling


Avoid:

- modern fonts
- sharp digital typography


---


## Background Elements


Allowed:

- tiny hearts
- small flowers
- hand drawn symbols


Maximum:

3 decorative elements.


---


## Universal Prompt Rules


Every generation should preserve:

"soft Morandi watercolor,
children's picture book illustration,
handmade paper texture,
warm ivory background,
large negative space,
gentle emotional atmosphere"
